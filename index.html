Flocks

Where the bird list comes from. Every named bird on the Target Birds fan wiki's release list (2016 to 2026), in the year and season the wiki gives, plus Target's newest birds that aren't on the wiki yet (Target.com, October 2026). Early unnamed birds, ornaments, multi-packs and accessories are left out. Each bird's page says where its photo came from. If you spot one that's missing, add it from All birds.

Featherly Friends, Wondershop, Hyde and EEK! Boutique and Spritz are Target brands. Flocks is a family tracker and is not made by Target.
